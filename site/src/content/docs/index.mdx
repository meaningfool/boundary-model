## How do you know you understand each other (you and your agents)?

A good conversation is one where you and your agent understand each other, one where you are presented with consequential decisions first and drill down once the foundations are solid. The trajectory of a conversation is quite chaotic and depends on decisions the agent made when gathering context and making assumptions. And there may be the appearance of a mutual understanding when it's actually a mirage.

The solution is a shared map, so that each new conversation starts from a known place.

## Focus on behavior changes first, implementation details last

Each step works one level closer to the code. You agree on what changes for the people using the system before anyone decides how to build it.

## Boundaries, level by level

A `boundary` is where a `consumer` meets a `provider`: what the `provider` offers, and through which interfaces. The first one sits between your users and your system. Open the system and the same pattern repeats inside it, between its components, one level down each time.

<Levels />

[The boundary model](/model/) covers the rest: what each `boundary` lists, how a `provider` opens into the next level, and the map that records it all.
